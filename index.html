Autumn in Whitebark Valley

The first book in the Whitebark Valley Series — a cozy small-town romance about Harper Bennett, the owner of The Hideaway bookstore, and Mason Sterling, the newcomer whose arrival begins to change the rhythm of Whitebark Valley.

Whitebark Valley Series · Book One

A newcomer. A bookstore owner. A small town where love has a way of changing everything.

Harper Bennett has built a life for herself in Whitebark Valley, where she owns The Hideaway, the town’s cozy bookstore. Then Mason Sterling comes to town.

As their paths begin to cross, Harper and Mason find themselves drawn into something neither expected. Against the warmth of The Hideaway and the close-knit rhythms of Whitebark Valley, an unexpected connection begins to grow.

Autumn in Whitebark Valley is a warm small-town romance filled with autumn atmosphere, bookstore charm, community, and a love story at the heart of Whitebark Valley.

About the author

Meet Tessa Orchard

✦

Tessa Orchard writes warm, heartfelt small-town romance filled with community, connection, seasonal charm, and characters searching for a place — and a person — that feels like home.

Her Whitebark Valley stories invite readers into cozy bookstores, familiar streets, changing seasons, and romances that grow in the spaces between everyday moments.
